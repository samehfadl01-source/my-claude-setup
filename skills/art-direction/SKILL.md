# Art & Visual Direction

Apply these rules before designing any frame, storyboard, or coded scene:

1. **Visual Hierarchy & Focal Point**
   - Every beat must have **one unmistakable focal point** (hero subject, key statistic, or product callout).
   - Keep on-screen text punchy: target **6–8 words max per beat** in ads and kinetic promos.
   - Maintain a clear typographic scale ratio: Headline `>= 2.0x` body size, subhead `~1.3x–1.5x`, metadata `~0.75x`.
   - Limit each project to **at most 2 font families** (one display/headline, one clean UI/body).

2. **Color & Contrast Discipline**
   - Follow the **60–30–10 balance**: 60% neutral canvas/atmosphere, 30% supporting brand surface, 10% high-energy accent reserved for CTAs and focal highlights.
   - Avoid harsh digital extremes (`#000000` / `#FFFFFF`) on graphics; prefer rich deep charcoal (`#0B0E14`) and soft off-white (`#F4F6F9`).
   - Enforce **`>= 4.5:1` text contrast**. Over live footage or AI clips, always place a subtle directional gradient scrim (`rgba(8, 10, 15, 0.55)` to `transparent`) behind text.

3. **Format Safe Zones (Non-Negotiable)**
   - **`16:9` (1920×1080 / 3840×2160):** Keep all vital typography and logos inside the central 90% width and 85% height (`>= 96px` horizontal margin, `>= 80px` vertical margin at 1080p).
   - **`9:16` (1080×1920 Reels / TikTok / Shorts):**
     - Top margin: **`>= 150px`** clear (avoid platform status/header overlays).
     - Bottom margin: **`>= 320px`** clear (avoid captions, handles, and audio ticker).
     - Right margin: **`>= 120px`** clear (avoid like/comment/share stack).
     - Left margin: **`>= 64px`** clear.
   - **`1:1` / `4:5` Social Feed:** Keep at least `80px` outer breathing room on all sides.

4. **Depth, Texture & Visual Cohesion**
   - Build scenes in **3 depth planes**: atmospheric background (soft gradient/footage/depth blur), sharp hero mid-ground, and subtle foreground framing or light particles.
   - Match color temperature, black levels, and film grain across AI clips and coded motion graphics so cuts feel unified rather than pasted together.
